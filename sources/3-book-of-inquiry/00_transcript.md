- Decision problems
- Design problems

Examples:

Should I optimize for money, freedom, health, meaning, status, peace, skill, friendship?

## 2. Coordination problems

These ask:

> How do multiple people act together despite different incentives?

Examples:

Teams, companies, politics, family conflict, communities, institutions.

These require trust, incentives, communication, and negotiation.

## 3. Identity problems

These ask:

> Who am I becoming if I choose this repeatedly?

Examples:

Career direction, habits, ambition, discipline, lifestyle, moral choices.

These are dangerous to reduce only to “evidence” or “optimization.”

Adding these would make the book much more human.
