*Companion to The Book of Inquiry. One page. Consult at the start of
any problem that survives a first shrug. If a box cannot be filled,
that box is the problem.*

## 1. What type of problem is this?

☐ Truth — *what is the case?*
☐ Decision — *what should I do?*
☐ Design — *how should it work?*
☐ Debugging — *why is it broken?*
☐ Coordination — *how do people with different incentives act together?*
☐ Orientation — *what matters; who does this make me?*

> If Orientation: put this card down. Articulate the values in plain
> words, watch your own behavior, design a small reversible experiment
> in living. The rest of the card does not apply.
