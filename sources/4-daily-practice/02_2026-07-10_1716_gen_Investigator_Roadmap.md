# Investigator Roadmap

## Purpose

Become a better investigator rather than simply acquiring more
knowledge.

## Daily (20--40 min)

### Architecture Rediscovery (15 min)

Choose one concept (React, GraphQL, Git, SQLite, Redis, Virtual DOM,
TypeScript). Ask: - What problem existed? - How would I solve it? - Why
would that fail? - What abstraction solved it? - What invariant was
introduced? - What tradeoff was accepted?

### Prediction Journal (5 min)

Record: - Claim - Probability - Deadline - Evidence - Falsifier

### Reflection (5 min)

-   What changed my mind today?
-   Where did I waste attention?

## Weekly

-   Resolve predictions.
-   Fill failure log.
-   Write one architecture essay.
-   Review one mistake.
-   Check whether the Book needs amendment.

## Monthly

Historical rediscovery: Darwin, Einstein, Shannon, Turing, Wegener,
Semmelweis, Marshall.

Review calibration. Review attention allocation.

## Quarterly

Reverse engineer one major system: Databases, Compilers, Networking, AI
Agents, Distributed Systems.

## Reading Curriculum

### Thinking, Fast and Slow

Focus: - System 1 vs System 2 - Biases Journal: - Which bias did I
observe? - Which Book principle counters it?

### The Scout Mindset

Focus: - Reality over identity. Journal: - Where did I defend rather
than investigate?

### Superforecasting

Focus: - Calibration - Updating Journal: - Which prediction surprised
me?

### The Structure of Scientific Revolutions

Focus: - Paradigms - Anomalies Journal: - Which assumption in my field
is invisible?

### The Beginning of Infinity

Read critically. Journal: - Which principle strengthens or weakens my
Book?

### Thinking in Systems

Focus: - Feedback - Stocks - Flows Journal: - Which hidden feedback loop
did I notice?

### Decisive

Focus: - Better decisions.

### Algorithms to Live By

Focus: - Search - Attention - Stopping rules.

## Reading Protocol

Before: - What problem is the author solving? - How would I solve it?

During: - What representation changed? - What invariant emerged? - Which
assumption disappeared?

After: - What changed my mind? - Does my Book require amendment? - What
experiment can I run this week?

## Success Metrics

Monthly: - Calibration - Changed my mind - Prediction quality -
Constraint detection - Representation quality - Attention allocation -
Learning rate

## Final Principle

The Book of Inquiry is the constitution. The Operating Card is the
workflow. The Inquiry Logs are the memory. This roadmap is the training.
Reality is the teacher.
