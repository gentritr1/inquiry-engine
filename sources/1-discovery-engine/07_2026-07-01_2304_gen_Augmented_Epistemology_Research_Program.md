# Augmented Epistemology

## A Research Program for Human--AI Discovery

> "The goal is not to build a smarter AI. The goal is to build a better
> process for producing reliable knowledge."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mission

Design, test, and continuously improve a research methodology that
enables humans and AI, working together, to produce explanations that
are:

-   More accurate
-   More predictive
-   More transferable across domains
-   More self-correcting
-   More resistant to elegant falsehoods

This is not a theory of civilization.

It is a theory of **how to discover better theories**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# North Star

A successful methodology should outperform:

-   humans reasoning alone,
-   AI reasoning alone,
-   conventional brainstorming,
-   unstructured research.

Success is measured by empirical performance---not elegance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Principles

1.  Reality has veto power.
2.  Every theory competes with alternatives.
3.  Unknowns are outputs, not failures.
4.  Prediction matters more than explanation.
5.  Mechanisms matter more than patterns.
6.  Revision is a feature, not a weakness.
7.  Confidence tracks evidence.
8.  History is a benchmark, not merely inspiration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Research Loop

Observe → Build evidence ledger → Define ontology → Map assumptions →
Find anomalies → Change representation → Generate competing theories →
Demand mechanisms → Apply constraints → Produce predictions → Design
falsification → Test against reality → Update confidence → Improve the
methodology itself

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 --- Foundations

Create: - Principles - Evidence grading - Confidence calibration -
Prediction template - Failure journal

Deliverable: A documented research operating system.

Success: Independent users can follow the process consistently.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 1 --- Historical Rediscovery

Objective: Determine whether the methodology reconstructs historical
breakthroughs.

Cases: - Darwin - Einstein - Shannon - Turing - Wegener - Semmelweis -
Marshall - Keynes/Hayek (competing theories)

Rules: Use only evidence available before the discovery.

Metrics: - Key anomaly found? - Wrong assumptions identified? -
Mechanism proposed? - Similar prediction? - False positives?

Pass: Repeatedly converges toward the historical insight better than
unguided reasoning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 2 --- Cross-Domain Validation

Run unchanged methodology on:

-   Physics
-   Biology
-   Economics
-   AI
-   Software
-   Medicine
-   Psychology
-   Law
-   Business
-   History

Question: Which steps remain universal? Which require adaptation?

Output: Universal core vs. domain-specific modules.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 3 --- Prospective Prediction

Produce predictions for: - 6 months - 1 year - 3 years - 5 years

Requirements: - Specific - Dated - Observable - Falsifiable

Review schedule: Every six months.

Maintain: Prediction scorecard.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 4 --- Failure Learning

For every failed prediction record:

-   Failed assumption
-   Missing evidence
-   Wrong mechanism
-   Representation issue
-   Constraint ignored
-   Method improvement

Goal: Improve the process---not defend the theory.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 5 --- Human--AI Collaboration

Assign specialized roles:

Generator Historian Skeptic Statistician Economist Engineer Scientist
Philosopher Prediction Auditor Synthesizer

Measure whether structured collaboration outperforms a single model.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 6 --- Discovery Benchmark

Research question:

Can this methodology discover hypotheses that experts later validate?

Evaluation:

Historical rediscovery

Prospective predictions

Cross-domain transfer

Independent replication

Lower false-positive rate

Better calibration

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Metrics

## Prediction Accuracy

Percentage of predictions confirmed.

## Calibration

Do 80% confidence predictions occur \~80% of the time?

## Compression

Does a theory explain more with fewer assumptions?

## Transferability

Does the same process work across disciplines?

## Revision Quality

Does the methodology improve after failure?

## Discovery Rate

Does it produce useful hypotheses earlier than baseline approaches?

# Success Criteria

The project succeeds if:

✓ It rediscovers historical breakthroughs from pre-breakthrough
evidence.

✓ It makes prospective predictions that outperform naive baselines.

✓ Independent users reach similar conclusions using the methodology.

✓ The process improves measurably after failures.

✓ It transfers across unrelated disciplines.

✓ It remains intellectually honest by reducing confidence when evidence
weakens.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Vision

The endpoint is not a grand unified theory.

The endpoint is a continuously improving research discipline.

Its purpose is to help humans and AI discover, evaluate, and revise
knowledge together.

Every iteration should leave behind:

-   better questions,
-   better experiments,
-   better predictions,
-   and a better methodology than the one that existed before.

That---not any single theory---is the real artifact.
